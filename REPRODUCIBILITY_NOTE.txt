Applied Econometrics — Reproducibility Notes
===========================================

This project uses {renv} for dependency management.

IMPORTANT WORKFLOW RULES
------------------------

• Whenever you install, update, or remove an R package:
    renv::snapshot(type = "all")

• On a new machine or after cloning the project:
    renv::restore()

• Do NOT rely on the global R library.
• Do NOT delete renv.lock or the renv/ directory.

Notes
-----
- Some packages may appear as "recorded but not used" in renv::status().
  This is normal for large Quarto projects and can be safely ignored
  as long as renv::restore() reports a synchronized library.

R version: 4.5.2
