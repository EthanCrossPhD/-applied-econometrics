22  Modern Difference-in-Differences Estimators

22.1 Motivation

The problems of the two-way fixed effects estimator under staggered adoption motivate alternative approaches to difference-in-differences that explicitly account for treatment timing.

These modern estimators construct comparisons only between treated and not-yet-treated units, thereby avoiding the forbidden comparisons embedded in TWFE.

In this chapter, we introduce two influential approaches:

  • Callaway and Sant’Anna (2021)
  • Sun and Abraham (2021)

22.2 Data Generating Process

We use the same staggered adoption setting as in the previous chapter to highlight the contrast with TWFE.

22.3 Callaway and Sant’Anna (2021)

The estimator proposed by Callaway and Sant’Anna constructs group-time average treatment effects, comparing treated units only to units that are not yet treated.

22.5 Comparison with TWFE

Unlike the standard TWFE estimator, both approaches explicitly respect treatment timing and exclude comparisons between already-treated units.

As a result, the estimated treatment effects align with the true data generating process.

22.6 Practical Guidance

  • Use TWFE only in canonical two-group, two-period settings.
  • In staggered adoption designs, prefer modern DiD estimators.
  • Always make treatment timing explicit.

22.7 Conclusion

Modern difference-in-differences estimators resolve the fundamental problems of TWFE in staggered adoption settings and have become the standard tools in applied work.
